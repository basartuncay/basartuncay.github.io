Ops Agent

For teams that want an AI agent to do real work in their systems, safely.

What it does
Staff give it back-office tasks in plain words: refunds, cancellations, address changes, restocks. It checks the facts through tools, acts, emails the customer and reports back. The same tools are available to Claude Desktop through an MCP server.
Where it's careful
The limits are in code, not in the prompt. Refunds over €50 and every cancellation wait for a manager, and the run resumes on its own afterwards. Impossible actions are refused, every step is kept in a trace, and text written by customers is treated as data.
Verified on every change: 14 evaluation cases check what changed in the data after each run, not the wording of the reply.

Sync Hub

For shops whose customer data lives in both the store and the CRM.

What it does
Keeps customers identical in a Shopify-style store and a HubSpot-style CRM, in both directions: signups, edits, order totals, VIP flags. Connects over OAuth and signed webhooks.
Where it's careful
When both systems change the same field, it overwrites neither and asks a person. Its own writes never bounce back and forth. If a system is down, changes retry, then wait in a list you can replay. Nothing is lost.
Verified on every change: OAuth, edits on both sides at once, a conflict, an outage and a forged webhook, end to end.

Revenue Pipeline

For teams whose revenue, payments and ad spend live in different tools.

What it does
Pulls Shopify orders, Stripe payments and Google and Meta ad spend every hour into one warehouse built with dbt, and shows net revenue, ROAS and CAC per channel, refund rate and repeat customers.
Where it's careful
20 data tests run on every build, including store-versus-payments reconciliation. A build that fails any of them is never shown: the dashboard keeps the last verified numbers and says why. Late refunds correct the right past day.
Verified on every change: the warehouse matches the sources' own totals to the cent, and bad data, a renamed column and a late refund are each handled.
Code and design notes
55 seconds, no sound.

Stock Sync

For shops selling the same stock on their own site and on Amazon.

What it does
Keeps one stock count for Shopify, Amazon and the warehouse. Every order, shipment and delivery updates it, and each channel is told what it may still sell, with a safety margin and bundles worked out from their parts.
Where it's careful
When the last units sell on one channel, the other shows zero about a second later. If two channels still sell the same item, the order is flagged with exactly what's missing instead of being half-filled. A stock count is only corrected when the difference shows up twice.
Verified on every change: a sell-out, an oversell, a late webhook, Amazon throttling and a hand-edited quantity, end to end.

Paymatch

For finance teams matching bank payments to invoices every morning.

What it does
Reads the bank statement (CAMT.053 or the bank's CSV), matches each payment to open invoices and books it in the accounting system. Then it sends reminders for what's really overdue.
Where it's careful
Every match says why. Discounts, bank fees, part payments and one transfer for two invoices are recognised. A payment is only booked on its own when the match is clear; the rest waits for a person. Nobody gets a reminder for money that may already have arrived.
Verified on every change: two statements in, six payments booked through an outage, two decided by a person, reminders to the right two customers only.
Code and design notes

Kickoff

For agencies and freelancers losing days between "yes" and the first working hour.

What it does
Turns an enquiry into a proposal, lets the client sign it online and pay the deposit with Stripe, then sets up the project board, a shared folder and the welcome email by itself.
Where it's careful
The signature is tied to a fingerprint of the exact text that was signed, and the client gets a signed PDF. Payments count only when Stripe confirms them. Reminders stop the moment they no longer fit, and setup steps can be retried without creating duplicates.
Verified on every change: enquiry to onboarded client by real email, through two outages, with forged and replayed payment messages refused.
Code and design notes

Lead Engine

For sales teams turning exported lead lists into conversations.

What it does
Imports lead exports, removes duplicates, checks each address, looks up the company and scores the lead with reasons. Good leads get a short, personal email sequence, replies are read and sorted, and interested people go to the CRM.
Where it's careful
Sending stays in office hours and under a daily limit. Anyone who replies, is out of office, asks to stop or bounces is handled correctly, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe. Nobody who said no is contacted again.
Verified on every change: two exports, real emails out, five kinds of reply in, and only the interested lead in the CRM.

Table Desk

For restaurants losing bookings between the phone, the website and paper.

What it does
One reservation book for an AI phone agent, the website, text messages and the staff. Guests get a confirmation and a reminder, can cancel by replying STORNO, and freed tables are offered to the waitlist.
Where it's careful
Two guests can never get the same table, even at the same second. Tables are joined for bigger groups, a retried phone-agent call never books twice, and guests' details are removed 30 days after the visit.
Verified on every change: a web booking, a phone-agent booking, a cancellation by text and a waitlist table taken with "JA", end to end.
Code and design notes

Chatline

For shops answering "where is my order?" on WhatsApp all day.

What it does
Answers order tracking and return requests on WhatsApp with buttons and lists, registers returns with the shop and sends the label. Everything else goes to a team inbox, where a person replies on the same chat.
Where it's careful
Order details go only to the phone number the order belongs to. The bot stays quiet while a person has the chat. It follows WhatsApp's rules: free replies only within 24 hours of the customer's last message, then approved templates, and shipping updates only for customers who opted in.
Verified on every change: tracking, a return, a handoff, the 24-hour rule, opt-in updates and a forged webhook, end to end.

Accessflow

For companies whose leavers still have a Slack account three months later.

What it does
Reads joiners, team changes and leavers from the HR system and keeps Google Workspace, Slack and GitHub in line: accounts ready the day before someone starts, groups moved when they change teams, everything removed after their last day.
Where it's careful
Admin rights are never given automatically: only the person's manager can approve them, from a signed link in Slack. Leavers' files go to their manager and their sessions end. A scan finds accounts nobody planned, and every change is in an audit export.
Verified on every change: a joiner, a mover, an approval, an immediate leaver removed everywhere in about 2 seconds, and drift found and fixed.

Meter

For SaaS teams charging per API call, document or minute.

What it does
Counts your product's usage, reports it to Stripe Billing and shows each customer's next invoice as it grows. Plans include a set amount of usage; beyond it, overage is billed per 1,000.
Where it's careful
Retried batches are never billed twice. Customers are emailed at 80% and 100% of what's included and can set a spend cap. A declined card starts a grace period instead of cutting a paying customer off, and our count is reconciled against Stripe's.
Verified on every change: usage counted once, matched against Stripe, a failed payment through grace and restriction, then paid and restored.
Code and design notes

Shipflow

For shops copying addresses into a carrier portal every afternoon.

What it does
Takes each paid order, picks the carrier by your rules, creates the label (with customs data outside the EU), prints the day's labels as one PDF, and marks the order shipped in your shop with tracking.
Where it's careful
Orders with a missing house number, a wrong postcode or a product without a customs code wait with the reason, instead of coming back a week later. A label is never bought twice, and parcels the carrier can't deliver or hasn't scanned for days come to you.
Verified on every change: six orders across four carriers, a held address fixed, one PDF printed, tracking back in the shop, a delivery and a problem scanned.

Reviewdesk

For restaurants and shops that mean to answer every Google review.

What it does
Reads new reviews for every location and drafts a reply in the reviewer's language that answers what they wrote. Praise is answered by itself; criticism waits for the manager to approve or edit.
Where it's careful
Anything about health, safety or legal threats triggers a Slack alert, and the draft takes it to a private conversation instead of discussing it in public. Reviews already answered in the Google app are left alone.
Verified on every change: eight reviews across two locations, four answered on their own, a food-poisoning claim held with an alert, an edited reply posted.

For teams answering the same customer questions every day.

Price Watch

For shops that need to know when a competitor goes cheaper.

What it does
Checks competitor sites on a schedule and keeps a price history for each product. It alerts Slack or Telegram when someone undercuts you, cuts a price sharply or runs out of stock, and emails a weekly summary.
Where it's careful
When a competitor redesigns their site, most scrapers report every product as removed. This one notices, changes nothing, and sends a single "check the scraper" alert.
Verified on every change, and tried on a real public catalogue: 11 products, 0 errors.

Invoice Platform

For finance teams typing supplier invoices into a system by hand.

What it does
Picks up invoices emailed as PDFs, including scanned ones, reads them, checks the totals add up and files them in an approval panel. Anything doubtful waits for review with the reason.
Where it's careful
The person who approves an invoice can't also pay it. Every change is kept in a history no one can edit. If a service is down, nothing is lost: it retries.
Verified on every change: five real emails in, each invoice filed with the right status.
