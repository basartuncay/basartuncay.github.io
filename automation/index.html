Ops Agent

For teams that want an AI agent to do real work in their systems, safely.

What it does
Staff give it back-office tasks in plain words: refunds, cancellations, address changes, restocks. It checks the facts through tools, acts, emails the customer and reports back. The same tools are available to Claude Desktop through an MCP server.
Where it's careful
The limits are in code, not in the prompt. Refunds over €50 and every cancellation wait for a manager, and the run resumes on its own afterwards. Impossible actions are refused, every step is kept in a trace, and text written by customers is treated as data.
Verified on every change: 14 evaluation cases check what changed in the data after each run, not the wording of the reply.

Sync Hub

For shops whose customer data lives in both the store and the CRM.

What it does
Keeps customers identical in a Shopify-style store and a HubSpot-style CRM, in both directions: signups, edits, order totals, VIP flags. Connects over OAuth and signed webhooks.
Where it's careful
When both systems change the same field, it overwrites neither and asks a person. Its own writes never bounce back and forth. If a system is down, changes retry, then wait in a list you can replay. Nothing is lost.
Verified on every change: OAuth, edits on both sides at once, a conflict, an outage and a forged webhook, end to end.

Support Desk

For teams answering the same customer questions every day.

What it does
Sorts incoming support email, drafts a reply from your own help docs and shows the source of every sentence. An agent reviews it and sends it in the same thread.
Where it's careful
Emails that try to manipulate the AI never get a draft. Urgent replies need a team lead. Questions your docs can't answer become a list of what to write next.
Verified on every change: real email in, the reply lands in the customer's mailbox as a threaded answer.

Price Watch

For shops that need to know when a competitor goes cheaper.

What it does
Checks competitor sites on a schedule and keeps a price history for each product. It alerts Slack or Telegram when someone undercuts you, cuts a price sharply or runs out of stock, and emails a weekly summary.
Where it's careful
When a competitor redesigns their site, most scrapers report every product as removed. This one notices, changes nothing, and sends a single "check the scraper" alert.
Verified on every change, and tried on a real public catalogue: 11 products, 0 errors.

58 seconds, no sound.

Invoice Platform

For finance teams typing supplier invoices into a system by hand.

What it does
Picks up invoices emailed as PDFs, including scanned ones, reads them, checks the totals add up and files them in an approval panel. Anything doubtful waits for review with the reason.
Where it's careful
The person who approves an invoice can't also pay it. Every change is kept in a history no one can edit. If a service is down, nothing is lost: it retries.
Verified on every change: five real emails in, each invoice filed with the right status.
